Verdict: NOT A WIN. Task 1 FAILS on this measurement.
  - Busy-phase wake latency (the phase where the bitmap should help most) is worse on all 3 pairs:
    p50 +32..+100% against 3.5% stock noise, and p99 +61..+131% against ~16-17% stock noise. That is a real regression, well outside noise.
  - rq_latency_ns on wake_llc is worse on all 3 pairs (+78..+165%) against ~6% stock noise. Also a real regression.
  - Idle-phase p50 is lower in 2 of 3 pairs (-60%, -26%) but higher in the third (+6%), and the patched values swing widely
    (19.75k / 52.25k / 35.5k ns) while stock stays tight (47.75-49.5k ns). Not consistent, so it is not a result.
    Idle p99 sits inside the stock noise (31.6%) and leans worse.
  - Remote wakeups: patched mean -0.7%. Pairs go +0.8 / -4.7 / +2.0% against 1.4% stock noise, so the sign is mixed.
    No consistent change.
  - Cycles: flat (inside noise). wake_llc instructions/IPC drop in 2 of 3 pairs, which fits the busy-wait engine
    spinning less while it sees more latency. That is not an efficiency gain.
  - wake_llc cpu_migrations are much higher on patched (63949 / 15466 / 108338 vs 5203-13638), and wake-target
    CPU changes rise too (wl_idle_cpu_changed_permille 147 / 0 / 298 vs 0 / 11 / 0). The patched idle search seems to scatter
    wakee placement and to behave differently from run to run.
  - general: everything sits inside noise, except cpu_migrations at -14.5% (consistent sign, but only just past the 12.4%
    stock spread). Treat that as marginal. general shows no regression.

Caveats
  - 3 runs per side, all taken within about 7 minutes of a fresh boot, with no other jobs running.
  - --force was passed to baseline.sh. No runs/BASELINE-task1 existed before, so nothing was replaced.
    runs/BASELINE and runs/BASELINE-llc-idle were not touched.
  - Final state: GRUB_DEFAULT = 7.2.9-llc-idle, which is booted now. Backup of the original grub defaults: /etc/default/grub.bak-task1.
