Roadmap ask
  "Replace the hashed folio wait table where collisions herd."
  Impact: Medium, only where many unrelated folios block at once.
  What exists: folio waiters share 256 hashed queues (folio_wait_table in
  mm/filemap.c). Comment: intentional space vs thundering-herd tradeoff.
  Better mechanism named: a per-folio wait queue (costs a spinlock on every folio).
  Workload: Database, storage.

Survey (full notes: reports/task15-survey.txt)
  PAGE_WAIT_TABLE_BITS=8 → 256 wait_queue_head_t buckets.
  folio_waitqueue(folio) = &folio_wait_table[hash_ptr(folio, 8)].
  wake_page_function() already filters with wake_page_match() — unrelated
  waiters on the same bucket are walked under the bucket spinlock but are
  NOT woken. Classic false-wakeup herd is filtered; remaining collision cost
  is shared spinlock contention + walking foreign wait entries.
  struct folio has no embedded wait_queue_head_t; layout is page-union
  constrained. Existing knob vm.page_lock_unfairness=5 is lock-steal only,
  not a wait-table size knob.

Decision (Approach A — measure stock; no gated per-folio WQ)
  A real per-folio wait queue is a structural folio-size tax the roadmap
  itself flags. Ship it only if collision-stress measurement shows a clear
  same-sign herd win. Measurements do not.

How to read
  Stock 3× ops/s spread ≈ 5.5% (2106–2228) — that is the noise floor on this
  disk-bound fdatasync path (p50 ~2–4 ms, p99 8 ms; log2 latency buckets).
  HIGH (1024 files) vs LOW (12 files): ops/s +6.7% for HIGH, same p50/p99
  bucket. That is inside/at stock noise and the wrong sign for a "collision
  herd hurts" story (more unique folios did not slow wait completion).
  Path is exercised: errors=0, pgpgout moves, wait latencies are real
  writeback waits — not a no-op harness.

Verdict: PASS (leave-alone / measured)
  Keep the hashed folio_wait_table. wake_page_match already prevents classic
  false wakeups; remaining bucket-walk cost is not visible above storage
  latency on this host under a many-unrelated-folio writeback storm.
  A per-folio wait queue remains a space tax without a proven efficiency win
  here. No kernel patch. No default change.
