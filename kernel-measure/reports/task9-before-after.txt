What the patch actually changes
  - Gate: sysctl kernel.io_uring_lockless_submit (default 0) + static key.
    Feature off => stock path unchanged.
  - When on and io_lockless_submit_ok(ctx): io_uring_enter() calls
    io_submit_sqes() WITHOUT taking ctx->uring_lock (SQ head publish / submit
    hot path). Deferred local task work still takes the mutex afterward.
  - SQPOLL / SYSCALL_IOPOLL / non-SINGLE_ISSUER / non-LOCKLESS_CQ unchanged.
  - Ring ABI unchanged.
  - Known limit: concurrent uring_lock takers (io-wq UNLOCKED issue, cancel/
    register from another task) can race while feature is ON — leave OFF except
    under the single-issuer DEFER_TASKRUN hammer. Defaults OFF for that reason.

How to enable
  sudo sysctl -w kernel.io_uring_lockless_submit=1

Verdict: WEAK WIN on submission throughput — not a claimed large win.
  - wl_nops_per_sec rises on every pair (+0.62..+0.97pct) and clears stock
    run-to-run noise (~0.47pct). Same-sign on all 3.
  - Derived cycles/nop falls ~0.6-1.0pct on every pair (same story: more NOPs
    for the same busy cycles).
  - Primary cycles and wall_time_sec are flat: the workload burns a fixed
    12s of CPU; unlocking the mutex does not free wall time, it raises NOP
    rate inside that window.
  - cache_misses are too noisy (~22pct stock swing) to claim anything.
  - Absolute gain is small (~0.8pct NOP throughput). Matches a lightly
    contended uncontended-mutex elision on a single-threaded enter loop.
  - Feature remains default OFF; do not ship as-is without the upstream
    submitter-suspend mutual-exclusion work for non-submitter lock holders.
